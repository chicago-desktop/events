# Where the pictures come from

`32/event_viewer.png` and `16/event_viewer.png` are this module's own pixel
art, drawn by `tools/event_icons.py` (`python3 tools/event_icons.py`) and
released under the module's licence.

The picture is a log sheet with three entries, each with the mark of its
kind — a red error, a yellow warning, a blue information — the three kinds
the classic Event Viewer listed. The file name is the picture's name in the
pack, `chicago.events:images/event_viewer`.

Until 2026-09-16 the module carried a copy of the shell's interim
`text_document` icon (Microsoft's artwork from `shell32.dll`); it is gone.
